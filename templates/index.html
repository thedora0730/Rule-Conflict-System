What is Śāstra Rule Precedence?

Śāstra Rule Precedence is a rule hierarchy inspired by Indian Knowledge Systems. When two or more rules overlap, the system determines which rule should take precedence based on predefined principles such as Emergency, Exception, Specific, General and Default.
